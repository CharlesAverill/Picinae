Profile findings

All six eligible proofs were profiled separately on both backends, after all
uninstrumented samples, with Set Ltac Profiling AND Set Ltac2 In Ltac1 Profiling.
Profiler compiles never overwrite timed inputs or artifacts and never enter
medians.csv/runs.json. profile-summary.csv lists every profiler return code,
compiler wall, accumulated tactic total and selected inclusive percentages.
profile-rows.csv preserves the final flat table's displayed rows (some labels
are truncated by Rocq). Empty summary cells mean below the displayed cutoff,
not zero time. The raw logs preserve the full displayed tables and call trees.

For memset, Ltac1/Ltac2 accumulated tactic totals are 22.401/25.395 s. Symbolic
stepping accounts for 87.3/88.6% inclusively. Hypothesis simplification is
70.5/62.7%; psimpl_mark_all_in is 22.5/20.6%; to_ast is 12.6/10.7%; compute in H
is 18.5/15.8%, and goal compute is 7.2/6.9%. This places substantial cost in
occurrence marking, reflective AST production and the preserved Gallina
reductions, rather than just ISA dispatch. Reflective compute and the retained
AST/PSimplifier boundary remain visible beneath Ltac2's native controller.
For i386 strlen, tactic totals are 35.460/48.777 s; stepping is 91.1/93.8%,
marking 24.8/16.5%, and to_ast 10.7/5.2%. Functional interpretation/context edits
are visible beneath step_stmt/isa_step_stmt, alongside reflective simplification.
Existing per-instruction Time output is retained for every sample and summarized
in step-time-summary.csv; it covers the other proofs' key symbolic steps too.

These percentages are nested, overlapping attribution, NOT an additive budget.
Ltac2 Control.enter/case/progress/ifcatch, native functions, Ltac1 callbacks and
reflective reductions often describe the same elapsed work. In particular,
large Ltac2 "local" rows with callbacks/continuations must not be summed as
independent overhead. Do not add ISA, psimpl and compute totals, compare their
percentages as exclusive costs, or subtract tactic totals from uninstrumented
compiler walls. The additional Ltac2 profiler instrumentation changes cost:
i386 strlen's profiled tactic totals differ much more than its uninstrumented
0.67% wall difference. This pass identifies work locations, not a causal
explanation or an independent speed comparison. Rocq prints the accumulated
profile twice (explicit Show and final reporting); the summary reads one final
flat table and does not double-count it.
