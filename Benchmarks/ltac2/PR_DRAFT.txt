Title: Migrate simplifier and ISA orchestration to Ltac2

Body:
Existing psimpl forms and all five architecture step aliases now enter Ltac2
controllers while preserving Classic proof syntax, wildcard occurrence
selection, dynamic simplifier/ISA hooks, and explicit independent Ltac1 paths.
The controllers handle AST lifetime, dispatch, fallback/cleanup, interpreter
stages, branches, unknowns, and memory facts. A transparent <: signature check
preserves the new adapters through functor parameters; sealing exported the
signature's legacy defaults and silently bypassed the native backend.

Gallina reflection, its soundness proofs, and existing compute/lazy reductions
are unchanged. Untyped AST generation/scoped evars, occurrence marking,
interpreter setup/checks, dependent context edits, and replaceable hooks retain
Ltac1 boundaries. No new axioms or admissions are introduced. The known failure
when a later local definition depends on the simplification target is preserved
with context rollback. See Benchmarks/ltac2/README.txt for the exact boundaries.

Validation uses the local picinae-env opam wrapper (Rocq 9.1.1, OCaml 4.14.2,
rocq-stdlib 9.1.0). Committed logs record a clean root build including all three
regression modules and 11 passing harness bookkeeping tests. All six lifted
ARM/i386 modules and six proof files build on original master a87fc67; unchanged
Examples are the comparison inputs. riscv_addloop_proof.v:148 (startof_prefix)
and riscv_cfi.v:490 (missing VaM) fail on that baseline and are excluded.
